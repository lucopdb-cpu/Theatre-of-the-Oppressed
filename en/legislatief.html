Boal's methods
Legislative Theatre
From Forum to law · Boal · TONYC · The People Act
The theatrical route to legislation and policy change. Developed by Augusto Boal during his term as a city councillor in Rio de Janeiro (1993–1996), translated to the American context by Theatre of the Oppressed NYC (2013–present), and developed further in the UK by Katy Rubin — including an award-winning collaboration in Greater Manchester. A method in which voters become spect-actors in the legislative process.
