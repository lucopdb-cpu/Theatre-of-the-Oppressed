Boal's methods
Image Theatre
The language of the body · Peru 1973 · Sinaletic · Sculpting · Dynamisation
Image Theatre came into being in 1973, during Boal's work for the Peruvian literacy campaign ALFIN, when he was working with groups who between them spoke dozens of languages and dialects. The impossibility of sharing words became the method: the body as first language, the image as signifier and signified at once. This carousel covers the foundation, the three-image architecture, sculpting and dynamisation, canonical exercises, the introspective Rainbow extension, the image sequence as a methodological addition from Dutch practice, and applications in research, climate and education — with Joker practice and pitfalls.

Image Theatre — method of Augusto Boal (1931–2009), developed during the ALFIN literacy campaign (Peru, 1973), elaborated further in Paris and Rio and reshaped by Bárbara Santos / Kuringa (Berlin), Jana Sanskriti (Sanjoy Ganguly, West Bengal) and David Diamond / Theatre for Living (Vancouver). The image-sequence method in the Sequence category is a methodological addition from Dutch practice — A Joker's Arsenal.
