Boal's methods
Forum Theatre
The heart of Boal's method · Anti-model · Spect-actor · Joker
The best-known and most influential form within the Theatre of the Oppressed. Developed by Augusto Boal in Brazil and Peru (1970s), spread worldwide and reshaped locally — from Jana Sanskriti in West Bengal (open-air Forums for thousands) to Cardboard Citizens in London (Forum with and for homeless people, Adrian Jackson) and Shadow Liberation in Bangalore (participatory shadow theatre). This carousel covers method, dramaturgy, Joker practice, variations in scale and pitfalls.
