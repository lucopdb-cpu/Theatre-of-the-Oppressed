Newspaper Theatre — Boal's techniques
Augusto Boal · Theatre of the Oppressed · 1974/1985
Ten techniques that Boal describes in Theatre of the Oppressed, with five cards of this Arsenal's own. Newspaper Theatre began at the Teatro de Arena in São Paulo, when the dictatorship in Brazil made popular theatre impossible: forms that people could make themselves (Boal, 2002, pp. 251–252). Not performing the news, but questioning it. Every article carries an ideology. The technique makes it visible.
