Paulo Freire — The suitcase
Nine techniques for the Joker
Freire had no exercises in the theatrical sense of the word — he had a method. Nine cards: some techniques he developed directly with rural workers in Brazil, and a few present-day Joker applications of his principles. Directly translatable into the practice of the Joker.
