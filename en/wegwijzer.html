How it fits together

The home page roughly follows the tree of the Theatre of the Oppressed: from the soil to the crown, and what grows around it.

  • Attitude
    Joker practice: what you do as a Joker, and how you relate to a group and to traditions you don't come from.
  • Soil
    Paulo Freire: the pedagogy Boal's work grew from. Theatre lines, on Boal and the theatre of his century, is under construction.
  • Trunk
    The TO Arsenal: all exercises, with two ways in. The Workshop orders them by phase of a workshop; Boal's book follows Games for Actors and Non-Actors. Above it, Image Theatre, the basis of many other forms.
  • Crown
    The forms many people know: Forum Theatre, the Rainbow of Desire (introspective and prospective), Newspaper Theatre, Invisible Theatre and Legislative Theatre.
  • Practice
    The Theateratelier (in Dutch) of Formaat in Rotterdam, and the Atlas with practices and regional deep dives worldwide.
  • Related
    Methods alongside TO, for now in Dutch: Playback Theatre, Psychodrama, Impro, Feldenkrais, and Theatre in Prison.

Reading a chapter

A chapter is a series of cards. With Step by step you see one card at a time; move on with the arrows, the arrow keys, or by swiping on your phone. With Overview you see all cards in a row and choose where to start.

The buttons above the cards show a part of them, in Forum Theatre for instance only the cards on the Joker or on ethics. At the bottom you'll find where the content comes from.

Every card and every exercise has its own address. Whatever you share or save opens later in exactly that place.

What the buttons do

At the top of every page, and on the cards themselves.

  • Chapters. The three lines at the top left open a menu with all chapters, in the order of the home page.
  • Search. The magnifying glass searches the whole site: exercises, cards and sources. Shortcut /.
  • My selection. With + select you keep an exercise or card in your own list. You can reorder that list, print it, copy it or send it as a link to a colleague. It stays on your own device; nobody else sees it.
  • Share. Sends the link to a single card or exercise, for instance to your co-Joker.
  • ↻ Random. In the TO Arsenal: let chance pick an exercise. Shortcut R.
  • Light or dark. The sun and moon switch the screen. Without a choice of your own, the site follows your device.
  • NL Nederlands. The NL button takes you to the Dutch version of the page.
  • On your phone. Put the Arsenal on your home screen as an app, usable without internet in a gym or community centre. How it works.

What the Arsenal is not

Not a recipe book and not a script. An exercise never works the same everywhere; that depends on the source group, the moment and the conversation afterwards. The site offers, the Joker chooses.

The Arsenal is also unfinished, and it looks with a Dutch and European eye. More about the why is in the foreword on the home page.
