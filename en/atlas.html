Atlas — practices in context
12 places · Rio · Medellín · NYC · Vancouver · Paris · London · Berlin · Rotterdam · Maputo · Dakar · Nairobi · Calcutta
Every cultural and political context asks for, and makes, its own adaptations of the method. This atlas brings 12 practices into view — not a directory of organisations, but a cross-section of what Theatre of the Oppressed has become in specific communities. From rural Forum in Bengal to ex-combatant theatre in Colombia, from work on homelessness in London to environmental Forum in Senegal. Not complete. But representative of the living diversity of the work. For TO in the Middle East and North Africa — Ashtar, Combatants for Peace, Aryan Rezai and others — see the separate deep dive TO in MENA (in Dutch).
