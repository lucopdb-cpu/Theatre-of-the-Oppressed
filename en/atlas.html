Atlas — practices in context
14 places · Rio · Medellín · NYC · Vancouver · Paris · London · Manchester · Berlin · Rotterdam · Maputo · Dakar · Nairobi · Israel/Palestine · Calcutta
This atlas is not an overview from above. It grew out of thirty-five years of Jokering in the social domain, and out of a curiosity that was there from the start: what are others, elsewhere, doing with the same method? When Google suddenly appeared, I started searching straight away. When Formaat began in 1999, Ronald Matthijssen and I started collecting TO groups from all over the world in the Yellow Pages on Formaat's website. Boal included the Yellow Pages in the signature of his emails, and CTO-Rio referred to them in the first issue of Metaxis (2001). That searching led to encounters, to the ITO and to the places on these cards.

So the atlas is two things at once: partly fieldwork, partly desk research. I have visited eleven of the fourteen places myself; Berlin, Dakar and Nairobi I know only from sources. Each card says which of the two it is. What is here is seen from one position, that of a Rotterdam Joker, and is therefore partial. That is not a flaw to be hidden. Wherever possible, the practices speak for themselves, in their own texts and words.

From rural Forum in Bengal to ex-combatant theatre in Colombia, from work on homelessness in London to environmental Forum in Senegal: every cultural and political context asks for, and makes, its own adaptations of the method. For eight places the card also shows what that practice added to the method: how Jokers took the work further, during and after Boal's lifetime. For the wider TO practice in the Middle East and North Africa there will be a separate deep dive, TO in MENA (temporarily unavailable).
